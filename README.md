# HuntersofUndeath
FoundryVTT module for Hunters of Undeath, a PF2e digital supplement

The following should be kept in-mind:

Venatoris For any Thaumaturge player who wishes to take the Venatoris class archetype it is mandatory they attach the Venatoris class feature to their character.

Venatorius Judgement As of currently, I am not proficient in JavaScript, nor shall I learn JavaScript for the purposes of making Judgement work. I love myself too much. That being said, Foundry has trouble automating Thaumaturge outright. I highly reccomend for the sake of your sanity that you install PF2e Exploit Vulnerability and use its exploit vulnerability macros to force party members to be able to interact with Judgement as intended. Use the Share Weakness macro on party members to simulate Judgement's ability to share weaknesses and damage, as they are functionally the same mechanic!

Undead Slayer & Hallowed Necromancer Since the Hallowed Necromancer & Undead Slayer are vanilla archetypes that've been revamped/remastered by Hunters of Undeath, their pre-remastered vanilla counterparts are already within PF2e's compendium. To minimize confusion, any revamped feats and spells are marked with the letter X in their title to signify them as part of this modules 'Expanded' catelogue.
